# Privacy Policy

**RepoSync** does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Data collection
- No analytics or telemetry
- No advertising identifiers
- No third‑party tracking

## Data storage
- Git credentials (tokens/keys) are stored locally on your device using Android’s secure storage.
- Sync data remains on your device and in your chosen Git remote.

## Contact
If you have questions, open an issue on the GitHub repository.
